# Document structure

Contents: Order of parts · Details of each part

## Order of parts

The documentation consists of the following, in this order:

1. Title page (صفحة العنوان)
2. Supervisor signature page (صفحة توقيع المشرف)
3. Dedication (الإهداء)
4. Acknowledgement (الشكر والتقدير)
5. Abstract (المستخلص)
6. Table of contents (فهرس المحتويات)
7. List of tables (فهرس الجداول), if any
8. List of figures (فهرس الصور والأشكال الرسومية), if any
9. Glossary of scientific terms, Arabic and English (قائمة المصطلحات العلمية)
10. List of symbols (قائمة الرموز), if any
11. Project content divided into chapters or parts (فصول المشروع)
12. Results (النتائج)
13. Conclusion (الخلاصة)
14. Recommendations (التوصيات)
15. List of references (قائمة المراجع)
16. Appendices (الملاحق), if any

Items 1 to 10 are front matter (Roman page numbers). The chapters start the Arabic-numbered body.

## Details of each part

**Title page.** Must contain: "College of Computer Technology – Tripoli" and "Programming Technologies Department"; the project title; the statement "Graduation project submitted as part of the requirements for the Bachelor / Higher Diploma degree in Programming Technologies"; names of the participating students; the project year; following the layout in Template 1 (`templates.md`). Margins as in the rest of the project. Arrange the information in the order of the template.

**Supervisor signature page.** A form prepared by the department that carries all project information and the approval of the department and the college. The student does not design it; leave a placeholder or ask the department's form.

**Dedication.** May dedicate the project to relatives, or to any teacher or person.

**Acknowledgement.** May thank the people or institutions that contributed in any way to completing the project. The student has full freedom in wording.
- Note: Acknowledgement normally goes to people involved in completing the project, whereas the dedication goes to relatives or friends who are not involved in the project.

**Abstract.** A clear summary of the completed project: the problem, how it was addressed, and the results reached. Must not exceed **one page**. Write the word "Abstract" (الملخص) at the top of the page.

**Table of contents.** Lists the parts of the documentation in the order they appear, with the page number beside each part. This is the general index of the document. It starts the indexes and includes all titles of chapters/parts, main headings and side headings, each with its page number (see Template 2 in `templates.md`).

**List of tables.** Contains the table captions and the page numbers where they begin; titled "List of Tables" (فهرس الجداول). Format: caption .......... page, e.g. "Table 1 – ASCII codes for characters ........ 1".

**List of figures.** Same style as the list of tables, with "Figure" replacing "Table"; covers pictures, drawings, and figures with their page numbers.

**Glossary of scientific terms.** A list of the scientific terms used in the project in Arabic with their English equivalents; prepared in coordination with the supervisor.

**List of symbols.** Contains the abbreviations and symbols used, with their explanation and the units used; titled "List of Symbols" (قائمة الرموز).

**Chapters.** Each chapter starts on a new page. At the top of the page: the chapter number at the far right (Arabic) and the chapter title at the far left. The header of every page in the chapter repeats chapter title/number as described in `formatting-spec.md`.

**List of references.** Numbered list; see `references-format.md`.

**Appendices.** Referred to by English letters: Appendix (A), Appendix (B), and so on. Pages inside each appendix are numbered starting from 1 prefixed by the appendix letter: A1, A2, A3, …; B1, B2, …. For example, the first page of Appendix A is A1. Typical appendix material: questionnaire forms, use case diagrams and their components, system control-panel screens, website screens, source code.

## Suggested chapter skeleton for a software project

The department's sample table of contents (Template 2) follows the software life cycle. Adapt to the student's project; do not copy the sample's subject matter.

1. Introduction (overview, main project activities, preliminary / analysis / design / implementation & testing / maintenance phases)
2. Preliminary phase (existing system, problems, proposed system, its features and objectives, requirements, development methodologies and the one followed, feasibility study, schedule)
3. Analysis phase (analysis tools, fact-finding methods such as interview and questionnaire, use case diagram, functional and non-functional requirements, initial screens)
4. Design phase (system components, entity relationship model and diagram, normalization, referential integrity, data dictionary, UML design, screen design)
5. Implementation and testing phase (testing types: functional, data integrity, constraints, acceptance; implementation: languages, DBMS, techniques)

Then: Conclusion, Recommendations, References, Appendices.
